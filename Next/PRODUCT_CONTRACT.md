# Blackstock Next — Product Contract

Blackstock Next is not a collection of disconnected creator tools. It is one channel-bound operating loop for discovering, cutting, packaging, publishing and learning from existing video.

## Product promise

**Connect your channel. Blackstock finds the best existing-video opportunity, makes the strongest permitted cut, publishes the result and learns from performance.**

Blackstock never promises virality. It increases the probability of strong performance through evidence, channel fit, professional editing and a closed learning loop.

## Competitor capabilities Blackstock must unify

### Research / opportunity intelligence
Equivalent-or-better coverage than current outlier/trend tools:
- outlier detection relative to channel baseline
- trend velocity, freshness and saturation
- competitor/channel tracking
- niche and topic discovery
- channel-specific relevance, not generic popularity
- explanation for every recommendation
- confidence separate from score

### Creator intelligence
Equivalent-or-better coverage than channel-audit / AI-coach tools:
- persistent Channel DNA
- channel topic lock with controlled evolution
- successful topic clusters
- duration/formats that work for this channel
- subscriber/watch-time/revenue value where available
- actionable learning after publication

### Clip intelligence
Equivalent-or-better coverage than AI clipping tools:
- semantic start/end selection
- hook, payoff and context independence
- silence and filler awareness
- sentence-boundary correctness
- visual activity awareness
- format recommendation based on source and channel
- no quantity-first clip spam

### Editing quality
Equivalent-or-better coverage than modern AI editors:
- original audio/language preserved by default
- no synthetic voice or generated filler footage in the core workflow
- source-aware frame rate and resolution
- semantic reframing, not face centering alone
- professional captions
- audio cleanup where it improves the source
- minimal recompression
- no unnecessary watermark
- edits should look human, not templated

### Captions
Market-ready requirements:
- punctuation-aware segmentation
- maximum two lines
- mobile readability
- reading-speed control
- timing overlap detection
- auto-fix before blocking
- channel/style presets only after technical readability is satisfied

### Packaging
Equivalent-or-better coverage than title/thumbnail research tools:
- multiple psychologically distinct packaging concepts
- title and thumbnail must complement one another
- explicit viewer promise
- packaging quality checks
- later A/B result learning from YouTube data

### Publishing
Equivalent-or-better coverage than schedulers:
- direct YouTube upload of final permitted export
- resumable uploads
- private/unlisted/public default selection
- title, description, tags, category and made-for-kids status
- recoverable network failures
- no source upload to Blackstock servers

### Analytics / learning
- CTR and impressions
- retention curve and first-30-second behavior
- average percentage viewed
- views/watch time
- subscribers gained
- returning-viewer signals when available
- revenue where permitted/available
- convert metrics into next-action recommendations
- distinguish view growth from valuable-channel growth

## Product-level rules

1. Source-first. Blackstock does not create fake replacement videos when usable source is absent.
2. YouTube-only foreign source uses official YouTube-native workflows; no hidden downloader.
3. Local rendering requires user-confirmed rights/permission.
4. Auto-fix first. Quality checks should repair recoverable issues automatically.
5. Only hard blockers stop the user: unreadable source, rights not confirmed, impossible render, invalid authentication or an unrecoverable upload error.
6. Quality > volume. Blackstock should recommend fewer strong clips rather than mass-produce mediocre ones.
7. Scores must have evidence and confidence; no decorative fake AI scores.
8. The active channel owns all recommendations, learning and production history.
9. Every visible control must do real work.
10. No feature is considered complete until technical, UX, quality and business-value gates pass.

## Launch gates

Blackstock Next is not called market-ready until measured test data clears all of these gates:
- crash-free sessions >= 99.5%
- successful supported-file exports >= 98.5%
- core workflow completion by first-time users >= 90%
- resumable upload recovery verified end-to-end
- captions pass readability/timing checks after auto-fix >= 95%
- Blackstock edits win or tie human preference blind tests at a commercially meaningful rate
- no known hidden YouTube-download path
- legal/source workflow is understandable without documentation
- 1080p30/60 and 4K30/60 reference exports validated
- interrupted render, lost network, expired auth and app relaunch recover gracefully

## UX rule

The visible app remains simple even as internal intelligence grows:

**Dashboard → Trends → Schneiden → Content → Analytics → Kanal**

The user should never have to understand the internal engines to get a professional result.
